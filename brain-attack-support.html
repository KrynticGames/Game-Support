Brain Attack Support

Updated October 16, 2026 · Current browser edition

Brain Attack is a trivia and hidden-target game played against the computer. This page covers the current browser build, not an unpublished iOS app. There is no developer email inbox, live support team or online moderation service.

Playing

Choose Easy, Medium or Expert and Play Game. The first-time tutorial teaches soldier placement, two defenses, trivia and targeting. Daily Challenge offers ten trivia questions; Practice Combat skips trivia. Regular questions last seven seconds. The tutorial is untimed. Mouse, touch and keyboard answer controls are available.

Saving and reset

Progress, XP, personal records, recent/seen questions, local question flags, tutorial completion, difficulty and sound preferences stay in this browser when storage is available. There is no game account, cloud saving, online leaderboard or online human opponent in this build. Progress does not automatically follow you to another browser or device.

Progress → Reset progress → Confirm resets XP and personal records. It does not clear every preference, seen-question list or local flag. Clear this site's browser storage to remove all of those local records. That cannot delete copies you separately downloaded or information retained by the hosting provider.

Question flags and content

Flag question on this device records a category and question locally; it does not send a report to the developer. The game does not collect a typed note or attachment. Export creates a file on your device. No one receives it automatically.

The revised bank contains 9,675 questions after conservative content screening. Some questions and estimated difficulty labels may still be inaccurate; the full bank has not been independently fact-checked. Screening is not a formal age rating. The game uses stylized soldiers, missiles and explosions without graphic injury or gore. It is not certified for all ages or presented as a dedicated under-13 service.

Troubleshooting

Reopen the game if it stops responding. Use a current browser and rotate your phone if the layout is cramped. Turn sound off with the Sound button. Clearing browser storage erases local progress, so try reopening first.

Privacy and rights

See the Privacy Policy for local data and hosting information, and the Terms for the game and third-party content licenses. There is currently no dedicated private developer privacy-request channel. No response or investigation service is promised.
