Word Havoc Support

Updated October 15, 2026 · CrazyGames and current browser edition

Need help with Word Havoc? Email krynticgames@gmail.com. Include the game name, device, operating system, browser and version, game mode, what happened and steps to reproduce it. Send a cropped screenshot if helpful; never send your password or payment information.

Start playing

Choose Quick Battle to practice against the computer, Local Duel to share one device, or Online Duel → Find Match for an online opponent. Find Match uses automatic matching; no separate Word Havoc sign-in or room code is required. Both players need an internet connection. If no opponent is searching, cancel and try Quick Battle.

Player names and accounts

When available, your signed-in CrazyGames username and avatar appear automatically. Guests get a generated name. Manage your CrazyGames profile, password, account recovery or account deletion through CrazyGames. There is no separate Word Havoc account-creation or deletion screen in this edition.

Saved records

Open Player Record on the main menu, or Settings → Player Record. It shows cumulative wins, losses and draws and recent results. CrazyGames' Data module provides cloud saving for signed-in players and browser-local saving for guests. Outside CrazyGames, progress is browser-local. Saves are personal records, not a verified competitive ranking. Unfinished online matches are not guaranteed to resume after closing the game.

If a result is missing, check the game's save-status message and that you are using the same CrazyGames account. Wait briefly for synchronization before closing the page. Browser privacy settings, storage restrictions, network problems or disabled platform saving can prevent a save. Do not clear browser storage as a first troubleshooting step; that can remove guest progress and local blocks. Account/cloud-data requests go through CrazyGames' account/privacy controls; developer-held data requests can be sent to our support email.

Connection problems

Check your internet connection. Cancel a pending search before trying again. A disconnected match pauses while the game attempts to reconnect. If the opponent does not return within about one minute, use Find Match to start another duel. Closing the tab ends that connection. A rematch lets the same connected players play again.

Reporting and blocking

During an online match, open Menu → Report / Block. You can report suspected cheating or an inappropriate profile/word, or block the opponent and leave. Reports send a category and technical match identifiers; they do not include a typed message. A block applies to the opponent's current identifier in that browser, not permanently to their CrazyGames account. Identifiers can change. Use CrazyGames' own tools for issues with a platform account or avatar.

Word Havoc has no player chat, private messaging, voice chat or player image uploads. Its offensive-word filter reduces inappropriate words, but can miss some variants. To report a missed word or dictionary mistake, email the word and the situation without including unrelated personal information.

Browser, mobile and accessibility

Use a current browser. On a phone, tap a rack tile and then a board square. The browser version supports mouse, touch and keyboard activation of board/rack controls. Settings include sound and reduced motion; a platform mute setting takes priority. If the display is cramped, try rotating the phone. If the game freezes, reopen it; an unfinished online match may end.

How to request data help

See the Privacy Policy for the information used by the game, providers, retention and deletion choices. Include an approximate date and relevant feature in a privacy request. Do not send passwords or sensitive identification documents in an initial email.
