Word Havoc Support

Updated October 16, 2026 · Current CrazyGames/browser edition

Quick help for a casual spelling battle game. There is no separate developer email inbox, support form or in-game report queue.

Choose a guest nickname

Open Player Record, enter a nickname and select Save Nickname. No separate signup, email address or password is needed. It stays in this browser while site storage remains available. Signed-in CrazyGames players use their platform name.

Play

Choose Quick Battle for computer practice, Local Duel to share a device, or Online Duel → Find Match. Online play needs two people searching and an internet connection. You do not need a separate Word Havoc account or room code. If no opponent is available, cancel and play Quick Battle.

Names and saves

CrazyGames supplies your profile name/avatar when available. Guests get a generated name. Player Record shows your wins, losses, draws and recent matches. With the CrazyGames Data module available, signed-in progress is cloud saved and guest progress is saved on the browser. Outside CrazyGames, saves are browser-local. Check the save-status message before closing.

To start your record again, choose Player Record → Reset My Record and confirm. This resets the current player's game record; it does not delete a CrazyGames account, provider logs or backups. Use CrazyGames' account/privacy controls for its accounts and platform data.

Connection or display problems

Cancel a pending search before trying again. After a disconnect, the game tries to reconnect for about one minute. An unfinished match may be lost when a tab closes. Reopen the game if it freezes. Try a current browser or rotate your phone if the layout is cramped. Clearing browser storage may erase guest progress and blocks, so do not use it as the first troubleshooting step.

Leave an unwanted match

Open Menu → Block & Leave. The block is stored on that device for the opponent's current identifier. It is not a permanent account ban, and identifiers can change. There is no chat, private messaging, voice chat or image upload. The word/name filters can miss some spellings or deliberate evasion. No official age rating or all-ages certification is claimed.

Feedback and privacy

On CrazyGames, use the game's platform rating/feedback controls when available for bugs or word-list mistakes. Feedback can be reviewed through the developer's CrazyGames portal; this is not a private support ticket or emergency service, and a personal reply is not promised. Do not include passwords, contact details, screenshots of personal information or other sensitive data.

For CrazyGames account, profile, advertising or platform-data questions, use the contact and controls in CrazyGames' Privacy Policy. See our Privacy Policy for the game's own processing and save choices.
