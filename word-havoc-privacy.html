Word Havoc Privacy Policy

Updated October 15, 2026 · CrazyGames and current browser edition

This notice explains how Kryntic Games handles information for the current Word Havoc browser game. CrazyGames separately operates its platform, accounts and cloud-save service. Questions for the developer: krynticgames@gmail.com.

Accounts and public player information

You can play as a guest. This edition does not ask you to create a separate Word Havoc account or enter a Word Havoc password. If you are signed in to CrazyGames and its profile features are available, the game reads your CrazyGames username and avatar URL and displays them in the game. Online opponents receive the displayed name and avatar URL. Guests receive a generated name and a random connection identifier. The game does not receive your CrazyGames password.

Progress and settings

With the CrazyGames Data module enabled, the game stores cumulative wins, losses, draws, best-hit statistics, recent match results and preferences through CrazyGames. Up to 20 recent results are retained in the game save; cumulative statistics continue beyond that history limit. Recent results include the public opponent name, mode, result, time and match statistics.

For signed-in players, CrazyGames provides account-linked cloud saving and cross-device synchronization. For guests, its Data module saves on the browser. CrazyGames controls what happens to guest saves when a player signs in or changes accounts. The game reads the active account's save and does not intentionally merge another signed-in account's records into it.

Outside CrazyGames, browser-local storage is used for progress and preferences. Sound, reduced-motion and match preferences are saved. The browser also stores local block-list identifiers and a random tab identifier. The host browser may retain a local match-state snapshot; this snapshot is not included in the CrazyGames cloud save. Closing the game is not a reliable way to preserve an unfinished online match. Browser storage can be lost if cleared or blocked.

Online matchmaking and match messages

Choosing Find Match connects to our Railway-hosted relay using encrypted HTTPS/WebSocket transport. The relay processes random player and room identifiers, displayed player names, avatar URLs, connection status, moves, played letters, scores and the match state needed to connect two players. One player's browser acts as the game host and validates moves. The host browser manages the full match state. State sent to the joining browser masks the host's rack and the future tile-bag order.

Active rooms are held in server memory and removed when their connections end or the reconnect window expires. We do not use the relay as a permanent archive of game-board messages. Hosting infrastructure can process IP addresses, request details, timestamps and diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the service. Encrypted transport does not make data anonymous.

Reports, blocks and support

Report Opponent sends the room identifier, random reporter/opponent identifiers, selected report category, game version and report time to our relay. Report metadata is written to hosting logs. There is no free-form in-game report message, chat, private messaging, voice chat or image-upload feature. Blocking ends the current match and saves the opponent's current identifier in that browser; it is not a permanent account-wide ban and identifiers can change.

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. We use reports and support correspondence to investigate problems, respond to requests and protect the service. Keep screenshots limited to the issue and avoid sending passwords, payment details or unrelated personal information.

Providers, analytics and advertising

CrazyGames processes platform, gameplay and save information under its own Privacy Policy. Its platform may provide analytics or advertising independently of this game. This build does not include a separate external advertising network or the developer's separate browser-activity tracker. Dictionary validation uses the bundled word list rather than sending word lookups to an external dictionary service.

Railway hosts the relay and these support pages; see Railway's Privacy Policy. GitHub Pages hosts the public policy/support copies and browser build; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Requests for avatars go to CrazyGames' image host. Email support uses the developer's Gmail address. These providers may process technical information in countries other than your own. We do not sell the gameplay records we handle or use them for our own cross-site advertising.

Retention and your choices

Local data stays until it is replaced, cleared or removed by the browser. The game limits saved history to 20 results; this is a game-save limit, not a promise about a provider's backups. CrazyGames controls retention of its account/cloud data under its policies. Active relay rooms are temporary. Report/diagnostic log retention follows the hosting service's settings and limits. We retain support/report information only while needed for support, security, resolving disputes or applicable legal obligations.

You may request access, correction or deletion of developer-held information by emailing krynticgames@gmail.com. Describe the relevant feature and approximate date so we can locate the record. Reasonable verification may be needed; do not send passwords or identity documents in an initial request. We handle requests as required by applicable law.

To manage or delete your CrazyGames account or platform cloud data, use CrazyGames' account/privacy controls or contact CrazyGames through its Privacy Policy. The developer cannot delete a CrazyGames account. Clearing browser storage removes local copies and local blocks, but does not delete a CrazyGames account or cloud save. For data associated with an earlier Word Havoc username/password build, contact developer support; those older accounts are separate from CrazyGames profiles.

Younger players and safety

This notice covers the standard CrazyGames edition, not a separate Kids Site release. Use it only if you meet CrazyGames' applicable age and eligibility requirements; the standard platform is intended for users age 13 or the applicable minimum age in their country and older. Do not include personal contact details in a public username. The game has an offensive-word filter, but no filter catches every inappropriate spelling. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Updates

We update this notice when the game's information practices change and show the revision date above. CrazyGames and other providers maintain their own notices and choices.
