Word Havoc Privacy Policy

Updated October 16, 2026 · Current CrazyGames/browser edition

Kryntic Games operates Word Havoc. This notice covers the current CrazyGames/browser edition. The game does not ask for an email address, password, private message or support attachment. It does not offer a separate developer account, email inbox or support form.

Profiles and saved progress

You can play as a guest. Where available, CrazyGames supplies your public username and avatar URL; an online opponent sees them. Guests get a generated name and a random connection identifier. The game does not receive your CrazyGames password.

Progress includes cumulative wins, losses, draws, best-hit statistics, preferences and up to 20 recent results. A recent result includes the public opponent name, mode, time and match statistics. The CrazyGames Data module saves signed-in progress to its cloud and guest progress on the browser when available. Outside CrazyGames, saves are browser-local. Browser storage also holds a local block list and a random tab identifier. The game no longer stores a full online-match snapshot and removes that old snapshot from the current browser when opened.

Earlier Word Havoc builds offered separate username/password accounts and server-side statistics. Any pre-existing records from those builds are separate from CrazyGames accounts. The current game does not create those accounts, and Reset My Record does not delete any older server-side account or statistics. The existence and deletion status of such historical records have not been verified in this review.

Online matches

Find Match connects to our Railway-hosted relay over encrypted HTTPS/WebSocket transport. Random player/room identifiers, public names/avatar URLs, connection status, moves, letters, scores and match state pass through it. The host player's browser validates moves and holds the full match state; the joining browser receives its own rack with the host rack and future bag order masked.

Active rooms are held in server memory and removed when connections end or the reconnect window expires. The relay is not a permanent archive of current-edition board messages or player records. The current build has no in-game report submission, chat, private messaging, voice chat or image upload. Block & Leave stores the opponent's current identifier locally and ends the match; it is not a permanent account ban.

Providers and retention

CrazyGames separately processes its platform, account, advertising, gameplay and cloud-save information under its Privacy Policy. This build adds no separate external ad network or developer browser-activity tracker. Dictionary checks use the bundled word list.

Railway hosts the relay and support site. GitHub Pages hosts public copies and the browser build. Avatar requests go to CrazyGames' image host. Providers can process IP addresses, request details, timestamps and diagnostic/security logs, including in other countries. Encrypted transport does not make this information anonymous. Provider log and backup retention follows their settings and policies. Earlier versions may have written report metadata to hosting logs; disabling new reports does not erase those existing logs. We do not sell game records or use them for our own cross-site advertising.

Your choices

Player Record → Reset My Record replaces the current player's game record with an empty record. It does not delete a platform account, infrastructure logs or provider backups. Clearing browser/site storage removes local copies and local blocks but does not remove a CrazyGames cloud save. Use CrazyGames' account/privacy controls for its account and platform-data rights. Local data otherwise remains until replaced or cleared; recent match history is limited to 20 results.

CrazyGames game feedback can be used for ordinary game feedback where available. It is not a private privacy-request form. A dedicated private developer channel for requests about developer-held information has not yet been configured. Questions about CrazyGames' own information practices should use the contact options in its Privacy Policy. This notice does not transfer the developer's responsibilities to CrazyGames or limit any privacy rights you have.

Appropriate play

Follow CrazyGames' applicable age and eligibility rules. This is the standard platform edition, not a separate Kids Site release. Do not put private contact information in a public username. The word and display-name filters reduce inappropriate content but cannot catch every spelling, deliberate evasion or tampered multiplayer client. Stylized combat is non-graphic. No official age rating, all-ages certification or determination of compliance with children's privacy laws is claimed. The game is not presented as a dedicated under-13 service.
