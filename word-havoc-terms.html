Word Havoc Terms of Use

Updated October 15, 2026 · Current CrazyGames/browser edition

Word Havoc is a casual word game operated by Kryntic Games. These terms cover the current CrazyGames/browser edition.

Playing

Follow CrazyGames' terms, applicable age/eligibility rules and the game rules. You can play as a guest; no separate Word Havoc password account is required. CrazyGames manages its own profiles and accounts.

Fair play

Do not deliberately use abusive words, harass others, cheat, tamper with match messages or disrupt the service. The game uses a bundled dictionary and offensive-word filter, which can miss some spellings. There is no chat, private messaging, voice chat or image upload. Menu → Block & Leave ends an unwanted online match and blocks its current opponent identifier locally; this is not a permanent account ban. There is no in-game report queue or promise of individual moderation.

Records and availability

Online matches are casual and unranked. Personal records are not independently verified rankings. This build has no purchases, wagering or cash-prize competition. Rules, word lists and balance may change. Online play and saving depend on browsers, connections and providers; interruptions or lost saves are possible. Unfinished matches may not resume. Nothing here excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

The Support page provides troubleshooting and platform-feedback guidance. No separate developer email inbox or support form is offered. The Privacy Policy explains profiles, saves, temporary match messages, providers and your choices. CrazyGames separately manages its platform accounts, advertising and privacy controls.
